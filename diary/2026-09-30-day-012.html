Day 12 — Wed 30 Sep

Wake-up: 7:30 AM
In 1,875 · Out 2,290 · Net −415 · Protein 125g

7:30 AM — Woke up a little groggy, sleep wasn't perfect, but overall okay. First real day of actually trying to follow a routine.

Early morning — Hit one of those funky waves where I just feel horrible about my life — the "I'm 33, what am I doing, I'm in the same place I've always been" spiral. Tried to actively lift myself out of it rather than sit in it.

~8 AM — Got ready, did skincare and dental stuff, got dressed, and biked to work — deliberately easy pace instead of pushing it. The fall air felt crisp and it genuinely lifted my spirits.

~9 AM — Got to the office and knocked out a full hour of LF work before anything else, breakfast was just pita chips. Felt great to get ahead of stuff I'd been putting off.

9:30 AM–4 PM — Genuinely productive stretch — got started on or partially completed five separate projects. Took a proper lunch break with curry chicken thighs, read my book, which I've really been loving lately. Want to stay on this wavelength as long as I can.

~3 PM — Something annoying happened at work. A colleague who regularly demands updates with zero notice pinged me, I told him no, and he looped his boss in immediately, which felt like pressure tactics. I pushed back and told them both it was annoying — came across a little flippant. Got nervous afterward since his boss is actually involved in my promo process, so I followed up privately to soften it. Turns out he's leaving in two weeks anyway, which is a relief, but the fear of it affecting my promo colored the rest of the day.

~5:30 PM — Went to the gym, but with the promo stress sitting on me I didn't push hard — mostly just walked, but glad I stuck out the hour.

~6:30 PM — Easy bike ride home. Remembered in the gym that today's the last day of Q3, so I had credit card rewards to use before they expired — felt lucky I caught that in time.

~7 PM — Stopped at Lululemon and redeemed a $50 Amex rewards card. Decided against cooking dal chawal and used my resy credit instead at a Palestinian restaurant down the street — got my favorite fattoush salad and their musakhan chicken, both excellent.

~8:30 PM — Digestion walk after dinner, brought leftovers home, and had a nice long catch-up call with my sister.

~9:30 PM — Brought packages upstairs, started organizing dal into containers and washing things, general apartment cleanup.

~10 PM — Did an hour of life-admin — booked flights for me and my parents from Madrid to Marrakesh for our trip, used Bilt points (not a points-maximization genius, but happy with 1.25 cents on the dollar), and emailed our travel advisor.

Rest of the night — Watching episode three of the new Pakistani drama I'm excited about, maybe some reading, skincare, aiming to be in bed by 11.

What I realized

  • Choosing an easy pace on the bike instead of pushing hard actually lifted my mood more than a harder effort would have — worth remembering on low-energy mornings.
  • Setting a boundary and then immediately softening it out of promo-fear is a pattern worth noticing, even though it worked out fine this time.
  • A genuinely productive day and a funky, self-critical morning aren't mutually exclusive — today proved both can coexist.
  • I have a weekday routine that's finally clicking, but no real plan for weekends yet — that's the next thing to build before it undoes this progress.
