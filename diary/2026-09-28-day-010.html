~7 PM — Read my book, which was nice, and aired out the apartment.

~9 PM — Did some LF work.

~10 PM — Just relaxing now. One thing I really want to cut back on: how much time I lose to short-form videos. I want to actually use my time more intentionally.

What I realized

  • What I've been calling "just how life is" might actually be burnout — giving all my energy to work and having nothing left over is a pattern, not a given.
  • Being hyper-busy at work isn't the same as being effective, and it's not sustainable either way.
  • Reaching out to fact-check something I mentioned in passing was the actual boundary violation today, not my sharing it — worth trusting that read.
  • Naming "hero child" out loud in therapy, even without solutions yet, still counts as progress.
