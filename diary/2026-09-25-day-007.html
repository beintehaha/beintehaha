7 AM — Woke up on my brother's new couch. Very comfy.

~8 AM — Saw my niece!! She was startled to see me at first, but then we started being snakes and making sounds and she quickly warmed up to me. Snuggle butt. We did breakfast, hung out, and then I left for work.

~9 AM — Working out of the Chicago office. Had a presentation in the morning that I thought was shit, but my bosses didn't, which was great.

Lunch — My brother, sister-in-law, and niece came to join me at work. We had lobster rolls. I could tell they were impressed, but it made me feel uncomfortable, especially because my sister-in-law was pressuring my brother to get a job here.

Afternoon — Drove home, my niece went down for a nap, we relaxed for a while. One thing that disturbed me: the person I'd interviewed with, her team members, had 1:1s with a woman and a man on another team who refused all contact and treated me like I had no worth. That person is also 400+ lbs, and I was fixated on this because I was trying to eliminate her own worth. Got me in a bad cycle.

Also afternoon — My brother lost his tooth while eating a piece of pizza, and that made me feel more stuck — like my brother is struggling with obesity and can't take care of himself or take himself to the dentist, and it's financially hard. But it's the emotional enmeshment. I just tried my best to stay above the line, and enjoy.

Evening — Watched Materialists and had Thai food. Not going to lie, today was tough.

What I realized

  • Fixating on someone else's body to try to strip her of worth is a bad cycle I can catch myself falling into, and naming it is the first step out of it.
  • My brother's tooth breaking wasn't really about a tooth — it pulled up a lot about obesity, money, and how enmeshed I get in his struggles.
  • Discomfort at being "impressed at" by family isn't ingratitude, it's about not wanting to be a source of pressure between my brother and sister-in-law.
  • A hard day with real moments of warmth (my niece, lunch, the movie) isn't a contradiction — it's just what a tough day actually looks like.
