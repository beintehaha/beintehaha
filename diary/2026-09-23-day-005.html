Evening — Lifted in the evening. A little anxious about tomorrow — travel, and I'm behind on work — but trying to just control what I can: finished packing, ordered breakfast for tomorrow, planned to do a bit more work before bed. Trying to keep the anxious motion in check rather than let it run the night.

What I realized

  • I'm opening up more to my sister and parents, which feels like real progress, even if it doesn't extend to my mom the way I wish it did.
  • I notice myself mirroring my mom's habit of criticizing others when I'm around her, and I don't like it in myself.
  • Controlling what's controllable (packing, food, a bit of work) is the actual antidote to travel-and-deadline anxiety, not white-knuckling through it.
